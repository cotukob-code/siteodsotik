% Educational Program
\section*{Educational Program}
\noindent\textbf{Program Name:} Professional Driving Skills Development Program\par
\vspace{5pt}
\noindent\textbf{Program Overview:} A comprehensive modular curriculum designed to develop professional driving skills through simulation technology. The program focuses on vehicle dynamics, racing lines, braking techniques, and performance driving principles in a safe, controlled environment.\par
\vspace{5pt}
\noindent\textbf{Teaching Methodology:} Real-time online instruction with personalized feedback, video analysis, and structured learning progression from beginner to advanced levels.\par
\vspace{5pt}
\noindent\textbf{Program Structure:}\par
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,noitemsep]
  \item Module 1: Fundamentals of Vehicle Control
  \item Module 2: Racing Lines and Cornering Techniques
  \item Module 3: Braking and Acceleration Dynamics
  \item Module 4: Lap Consistency and Racecraft
  \item Module 5: Advanced Performance Driving
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Session Format:} 45–60 minute online sessions with live instruction, video recording analysis, and personalized development plans.\par
\vspace{15pt}
